*Distilled from 76 YouTube videos, 220,000+ words of transcripts*

## THE ESSENCE OF 108

108 Performance isn't just a baseball training facility. It's a **relentless search for truth** in human movement, athletic development, and what it means to compete at the highest level. Founded by Eugene Bleecker, 108 represents the bridge between old-school baseball wisdom and cutting-edge movement science—without ego, without dogma, just results.

---

## CORE PHILOSOPHY: "THE SEARCH FOR TRUTH"

### The Foundational Belief
> "I've been on a search for truth... It can't be about being right. It has to be about **getting it right**. We have to take the emotion out of it."

108 doesn't worship at the altar of "old school" or "new school." They test everything. They question everything. They've discovered that:
- The old-school guys were right about principles
- The new-school guys brought valuable data
- But **neither side has the complete picture**

### The Sphere of Truth
> "If you're looking into a sphere from any angle, everything you're seeing inside is an accurate representation. But change the angle five degrees, and all of a sudden you're privy to something new."

This is 108's epistemological framework: **multiple valid perspectives exist simultaneously**. Truth isn't a single angle—it's understanding the whole sphere.

## BRAND VOICE CHARACTERISTICS

### 1. RAW & UNFILTERED
The brand literally has a series called "Raw Takes." No polish, no corporate speak. Eugene will tell you when something is "garbage" or "dog crap." This isn't rudeness—it's **radical honesty in service of the athlete**.

### 2. PASSIONATE TO THE BONE
> "I love this game and I've loved this game my whole life."

You can't fake the energy. This comes through in every video, every coaching session. The love isn't performed—it's **visceral**.

### 3. INTELLECTUALLY HUMBLE
> "At this point I realized I don't know anything... The Dunning-Kruger scale is really more of a circle. You keep coming back around."

108 has the confidence to say "we've tested this" but also the humility to say "we might be wrong." This combination builds **deep trust**.

### 4. PERSON-FIRST, ALWAYS
> "Know their heartbeat before you know their swing."
> — Clint Hurdle, on the 108 approach

This isn't about producing robots. It's about understanding the **human being** inside the athlete.

### 5. ANTI-ESTABLISHMENT (But Not Contrarian)
> "Popular opinion is the greatest lie in the world."

108 doesn't reject establishment thinking to be edgy. They reject it because **they've tested it and found better ways**.

## METHODOLOGY: THE 108 WAY

### Core Training Principles

1. **Movement Creates Data** (Not the Other Way Around)
   - Don't let data craft the athlete
   - Use data to understand what the athlete is already doing well

2. **Efficiency Over Everything**
   - "Move cleaner, be more efficient, be more repeatable"
   - Velocity UP while stress goes DOWN (counterintuitive but proven)

3. **Long-Term Development**
   - Kids come in at 9-10 years old
   - Same kids get drafted in the first round at 17-18
   - "We're not changing what they do well. We're teaching them what to stay away from."

4. **The N.E.S.T. Framework** (Tony Abbatine)
   - **N**utrition
   - **E**xercise (and Hydration)
   - **S**leep
   - **T**ime
   - "Without the nest, there's no place to hold the eggs"

5. **No Constraints**
   > "I'm not going to have somebody hire me and tell me to do a job and then try to tell me how to do it. The constraints are—not having any is such an enormous thing."
