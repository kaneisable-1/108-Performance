## EXTENDED INTERNSHIP COPY (Optional Sections)

### Section: What You'll Do

**Daily Training Support**
- Assist with Tri Star group classes (hitting, pitching, RFD)
- Support 108 Experience athletes during assessments and training
- Help run Performance Institute training sessions
- Set up and break down training equipment

**Video & Analysis**
- Film training sessions and drill work
- Assist with video review and feedback
- Learn our movement analysis framework
- Support content creation for athlete programming

**Athlete Development**
- Work directly with youth, high school, and college athletes
- Learn the 108 methodology: Human -> Athlete -> Player
- Understand individualized programming through Bridge App
- Observe and participate in athlete debriefs

**Facility Operations**
- Maintain training spaces and equipment
- Support scheduling and athlete check-ins
- Assist with camps, clinics, and Tour Experiences
- Represent 108 professionally at all times

### Section: Expectations

**Be Early** - If you're on time, you're late.

**Be Coachable** - Accept feedback, ask questions, check your ego.

**Be Present** - No phones during sessions. Full attention on the work.

**Be Professional** - How you dress, speak, and treat every person matters.

**Be Curious** - Ask "why" constantly. Study on your own. Seek to understand.

**Be Humble** - You don't know everything. Neither do we. Trust the process.

### Section: FAQs

**Is this a paid internship?**
No. This is an unpaid educational experience. Top performers are considered for paid positions.

**Do you provide housing?**
No. We recommend local candidates or those willing to relocate.

**How long is the program?**
3-6 months. Longer commitments lead to deeper learning.

**What's the weekly time commitment?**
20-40 hours per week.

**When can I start?**
We accept applications year-round. Start dates are flexible.
